# Tilt Wave Studios outreach

Tracker: https://claude.ai/artifact/EDbiE7d3tMmoeRmwYkcYd9

- `leads.json` / `leads.csv`: 35 leads (D2C, startups, agencies, local businesses in Kolkata, Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR)
- `tracker.html`: the tracker page source

## Daily routine (15–20 min)
1. Open the tracker and click **Follow-up due** first, then **Not contacted**.
2. Pick a lead, read the message, click **Open in Gmail** (or **Open in WhatsApp**), edit if you want, and send.
3. Click **Mark sent**. The tracker schedules the next follow-up: email 2 three days later, then email 3 four days after that.
4. When someone replies, set the status to **Replied** and send the promised mini-audit within 48 hours.

## Keep your accounts safe
- Send about 15–20 cold emails a day from Gmail at most. Avoid sending in bulk.
- WhatsApp: only message numbers a business lists as WhatsApp on its own site. Send one message and one follow-up at most, from the WhatsApp Business app. Cold bulk messages through the WhatsApp API break Meta's policy and get numbers banned.
- Honour every "no thanks" or "STOP" right away by setting the lead to **Not interested**.
- Leads marked **listing** came from third-party directories, so check the contact before sending.
- Dental clinics: keep their video ideas informational (a clinic tour, meeting the doctor). Medical-practice advertising rules restrict promotional claims.
